FAQ

Questions

What is Type C Custom?

We design your artwork and print it on a USB stick or a card-shaped USB. You can put music uploads, music videos, albums and mixtapes, artist artwork and other content on it. It’s meant to feel like a modern CD — something you can own, show and play.

Can you design the artwork for me?

Yes. That’s the main service. You can also use Design with Nova: Layout places a photo, name and colours you supply; Imagine generates a picture from a description, for free, with no API keys (Pollinations.ai). Your upload does not change what Imagine draws — it can only appear as a small inset. Download the PNG and send it with your enquiry.

Does Imagine use my photo?

No. Imagine has no image-to-image step, so the AI only reads your written description. If you upload a photo in Imagine mode, Nova places it as a small inset on the stick and card. Generated pictures carry a small “AI-generated” note. The free service can be slow or fail when it is busy.

Who is on the shop?

Hoodlum Asylum is the launch artist, including Street Credibility and CODEAQUE × NOVA — NO COMPETITION. Other artists will be added when they join. We don’t list anyone who hasn’t signed up.

How much do they cost?

Prices are not set yet, so you’ll see “from £__” until they are. Pre-register and we’ll email you when ordering opens. Nothing is charged now.

How do I send music or videos with my pre-register?

Use the “Upload your content” step on the pre-register form. You can attach music, music videos, artwork and other files, but FormSubmit will only email attachments totalling 10 MB. Larger files — especially videos — should be sent as a Google Drive, WeTransfer or Dropbox share link in the field provided. Nothing is stored on this website; files that do go through arrive as email attachments at hoodlumasylumuk@gmail.com.

How is it shipped?

Small orders are made and posted from home in the UK, usually with Royal Mail. We aim to turn little orders around quickly. We haven’t published delivery times or international rates yet — ask us if you’re outside the UK.

Can I sell my music with Type C Custom?

Yes — use the artist interest form and tell us what you’d like to release. We’ll write back when we’ve read it. Sending the form doesn’t put you in the shop automatically.

What about the domain?

typec-music.com is one option, or another Type C Custom name if that fits better. Nothing is connected yet. This preview is the same website, hosted for free in the meantime.
